Interactive map

Browse every reporting station on a live, color-coded temperature map you can scrub by date.

📍

🕑

Full history

Look back through any station's past readings, not just the current snapshot.

Installing

  1. Unzip the download and drag Groundcheck.app into Applications.
  2. Since it isn't code-signed, macOS will block the first launch as "unidentified developer."
  3. Right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → confirm. After that it opens normally, every time.
