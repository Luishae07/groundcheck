# Credits for Groundcheck

Groundcheck is made with the work of many other people. This file lists them, in the same words as the Credits tab of the app. If you write about Groundcheck or use its numbers, please credit the data networks below as well (see how-to-cite.txt).

Site: https://luishae07.github.io/
Overview: https://luishae07.github.io/llms.txt

## Weather data

### sonde.mine.nu / zeesen.mine.nu

Live radiosonde telemetry from a public tracking network run by volunteers with their own receivers. sonde.mine.nu (https://sonde.mine.nu)

### SondeHub

An open, worldwide database of radiosonde telemetry, fed by hundreds of volunteer receivers. It gives Groundcheck readings from North and South America, Asia, Australia and more. sondehub.org (https://sondehub.org)

### NOAA IGRA

The Integrated Global Radiosonde Archive of NOAA's National Centers for Environmental Information: the official soundings of about 650 weather-service stations in more than 100 countries (public domain). NOAA NCEI (https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/weather-balloon/integrated-global-radiosonde-archive)

## Good to know

### Forecasts can be wrong

Measurements come straight from weather balloon sensors. The Next days forecast is an estimate that Groundcheck calculates from collected readings, with no weather model, and it can be wrong. Its typical error is shown next to it.

### Thank you

To everyone who builds, launches, hosts and runs radiosondes and receivers. Without them there would be nothing to show.
