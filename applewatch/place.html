Set your place

The Apple Watch page cannot ask for your location, so it needs to be told. Paste a Google Maps link to the place you want, and you get a link for the watch.

1. In Google Maps, open your place, tap Share and Copy link. 2. Paste it here.

Open it

Send the link to yourself in Messages, then tap it on your watch. Or save it in a note or a Shortcut.

A short link such as maps.app.goo.gl/… cannot be read here. Open it in Safari first, then copy the long address from the address bar. Links from OpenStreetMap, or plain numbers like 52.52, 13.40, also work.
